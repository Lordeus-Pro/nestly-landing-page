# Nestly — Landing Page Requirements

## Product

- **Name:** Nestly
- **Tagline:** Skip the meeting. Keep the momentum.
- **What it is:** An async standup tool.
- **Audience:** Remote team managers running teams of 10–50 people.

## Problem

Daily standups waste 30 minutes a day. Nestly replaces the live meeting with async check-ins so teams keep the visibility without losing the time.

## Features

- **3-question check-ins** — quick, structured daily updates.
- **Auto summaries** — automatically digest each day's check-ins.
- **Slack delivery** — summaries delivered straight to Slack.
- **Team health score** — a signal on how the team is really doing.

## Pricing

- **Free** — up to 5 people.
- **Paid** — **$12 / seat / month** beyond 5 people.

- **Hero:** Bold value prop + **"Get started for free"** CTA.
- **Problem:** Visualizing the "calendar graveyard" of daily meetings.
- **How it Works:** The 3-step loop: **Prompt → Response → Summary.**
- **Features:** Deep dive into Slack delivery and Health Scores.
- **Pricing:** Simple, transparent tiered cards.
- **Social Proof:** Testimonials from the front lines.
