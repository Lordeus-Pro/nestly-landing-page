How it works

One simple loop. Zero meetings.

Nestly runs the same tight loop every day so nothing slips and nobody has to dial in.

At a time you pick, Nestly nudges everyone with the same three questions. No app to open, no link to hunt for.

→
2

Response

Each person answers in under two minutes, whenever it suits their morning — coffee in hand, on their own clock.
